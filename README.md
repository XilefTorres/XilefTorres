| 🏭 **Untitled Management Game** | Management / Automation · 2D pixel art top-down | 🏗️ In Development | `Unity` `C#` `Aseprite` |

<details>
<summary><b>📜 What's it about?</b></summary>
<br>

> **Premise:** You're a financial fugitive running from your debts and the tax authorities. Hiding out on a piece of land you inherited, you start from scratch and build an industrial empire — with one goal: pay off everything you owe and start a debt-free life.

**Core mechanics**
- 🪓 **From manual to automated:** gather and craft by hand, then build machines, conveyor belts and sorters until your production runs on its own.
- 💳 **Credit history system:** paying down your debt raises your credit score, unlocking better tools, business contracts and new biomes.
- 🧾 **Tax risk meter:** the more you earn, the higher your audit risk — keep it low by paying taxes or face fines.
- 🏗️ **Build mode:** design and expand your factory layout freely.
- 🏁 **Victory condition:** clear your debt… then see how far your empire can grow.

</details>
